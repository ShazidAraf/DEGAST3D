DEGAST3D

Learning Deformable 3D Graph Similarity for Tracking Cells in Unregistered Time-Lapse Microscopy
Md Shazid Islam, Arindam Dutta, Calvin-Khang Ta, Kevin Rodriguez, Christian Michael, Mark Alber, G. Venugopala Reddy, Amit K. Roy-Chowdhury
GitHub Paper (IEEE TCBB 2025)

Overview

Tracking densely packed cells in 3D time-lapse microscopy is challenging due to non-rigid growth, cell division, imaging noise, and unregistered acquisition. DEGAST3D introduces a learning-based framework that models local cellular neighborhoods as deformable 3D graphs and learns graph similarity for robust cell correspondence over time.

Results

Performance summary:
• +6.4% F1 improvement in cell tracking
• +14.8% F1 improvement in cell division detection
• Robust to segmentation noise and long time-lapse sequences

Why This Matters

DEGAST3D shows how learning on structured 3D representations can replace brittle heuristic matching in biological tracking pipelines. The framework generalizes to other densely packed cellular systems such as organoids, embryos, and volumetric biomedical imaging.
